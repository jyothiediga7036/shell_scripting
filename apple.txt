#### What is Apple?

Apple is an American technology company founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne.

#### What does Apple do?

Apple designs and sells:

* iPhone – Smartphones
* iPad – Tablets
* MacBook – Laptops
* Apple Watch – Smartwatches
* AirPods – Wireless earphones

#### Why is Apple popular?

* Easy-to-use products
* Strong security and privacy features
* High-quality hardware and software integration
* Ecosystem where devices work together seamlessly

#### How does Apple make money?

Mainly through:

1. Hardware sales (iPhone, Mac, iPad, etc.)
2. Services such as Apple Music, iCloud, and the App Store.
